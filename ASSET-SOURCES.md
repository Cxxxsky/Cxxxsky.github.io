# Standalone institution symbols

- HIT: `hit-emblem.svg` is a native-path SVG conversion of the `hitlogo` vector in yuxtech/cnlogo. Original path shapes and color (#006b89) are preserved; no raster image is embedded. Source: https://github.com/yuxtech/cnlogo/blob/master/cnlogo/hit.tex . License text is included alongside the vector. The shape was visually checked against the official emblem at https://www.hit.edu.cn/11544/list.htm .
- BJTU: `bjtu-emblem.svg` is converted directly from the official standard-color emblem AI file, with the artboard cropped around the emblem. No raster image is embedded. Source: https://www.bjtu.edu.cn/xxgk/xxbz/index.htm ; archive: https://www.bjtu.edu.cn/docs/2024-11/f79a42a27f374de9b11630384f014cf1.zip .
- SLAI: transparent PNG containing only the graphic mark, losslessly cropped from its official homepage wordmark: https://www.slai.edu.cn/sites/default/files/2026-03/%E9%A6%96%E9%A1%B5logo_black.png . This is not claimed to be SVG.
- United Imaging: transparent PNG containing only the two-piece graphic mark, losslessly cropped from https://global.united-imaging.com/-/media/uih/logo/logo.png . This is not claimed to be SVG.

All institution/company marks remain owned by their respective organizations. Their use here denotes the site owner's affiliation/history, not endorsement.

Paper framework diagrams were supplied by the site owner. The visitor-map basemap uses public-domain Natural Earth land polygons; the disconnected placeholder contains no visitor statistics.
